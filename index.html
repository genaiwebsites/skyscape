About the Artist

Eyes in the
Sky. Heart
on Earth.

I'm Kshitiz Bathwal, a landscape and aerial photographer based in India — chasing the geometry of rivers from 400 feet, the symmetry of terraced fields at golden hour, and the chaos of cities that only reveals its order from above.

Flying a drone is my way of asking the earth a question it only answers when you stop standing on it. Skyscape Photography is not a studio — it's a point of view. Every frame is a reminder that perspective changes everything.

"Every place looks like nowhere until you see it from above — then it looks like everywhere." — Kshitiz Bathwal
